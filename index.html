Who I Am

I'm a Principal Software Engineer with 8+ years of experience turning complex business problems into clean, scalable software. My career spans the full stack — from Magento 2 storefronts handling thousands of daily transactions to AI-powered SaaS platforms built with FastAPI, RAG pipelines, and vector databases.

Today I work at the intersection of backend engineering and applied AI. I'm the creator of DocMind, a multi-tenant document intelligence platform built on FastAPI, PostgreSQL, ChromaDB, pgvector, Ollama, and Celery — giving teams conversational access to their knowledge base through retrieval-augmented generation.

I integrate AI development tools — GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, ChatGPT, and Cursor AI — into my daily engineering workflow as genuine productivity multipliers. I believe engineers who pair deep technical mastery with AI-assisted velocity are the ones who ship more, faster, and better.

Holding an MCA from Pune University and certifications in Web Technologies (C-DAC) and Cloud & DevOps (Learnbay, 2024), I stay at the edge — fine-tuning RAG evaluation harnesses, designing microservices, or deep-diving system architecture at scale.
